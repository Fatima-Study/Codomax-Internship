## 🧠 What I Learned

### Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science that enables machines and computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples include:

- Decision-making
- Problem-solving
- Speech recognition
- Image recognition
- Natural language processing

### Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every task.

Examples include:

- Spam detection
- Fraud detection
- Recommendation systems
- Predictive analysis

## 🌍 AI & ML in Everyday Life

Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are widely used in everyday technologies. They help automate tasks, analyze data, improve decision-making, and provide personalized services.

| Technology | Concept | Everyday Example |
|---|---|---|
| 🤖 Chatbots | AI | Customer support and virtual assistance |
| 🎙️ Voice Assistants | AI | Understanding voice commands |
| 👤 Face Recognition | AI | Smartphone unlocking and authentication |
| 📧 Spam Detection | ML | Identifying unwanted emails |
| 💳 Fraud Detection | ML | Detecting suspicious banking transactions |
| 🛒 Recommendation Systems | ML | Product and content recommendations |
| 🎬 Content Recommendations | ML | Personalized videos and movies |
| 🗺️ Navigation Systems | AI/ML | Route optimization and traffic prediction |

---

### 💡 Key Point

AI focuses on enabling machines to perform intelligent tasks, while ML allows systems to learn from data and improve their predictions or decisions over time.
